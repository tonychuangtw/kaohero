  1. 1The great ＿＿＿ of the Internet and social media makes it easy to spread false information.

    1. (A) consumption
    2. (B) reduction
    3. (C) popularity
    4. (D) deduction

    正解：(C)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (C) popularity 意為普及、廣受歡迎；網路與社群媒體的高度普及使假訊息容易擴散，語意連貫。

    ❌ (A) consumption 指消耗、消費，與訊息擴散的原因無關。

    ❌ (B) reduction 指減少，與「容易散布」的結果矛盾。

    ❌ (D) deduction 指推論或扣除，語意不合。

    📚 出處：英文字彙 popularity。

  2. 2Fake news can be a dangerous way of obtaining information; readers need to develop a(n) ＿＿＿ mindsetwhen reading news stories.

    1. (A) critical
    2. (B) efficient
    3. (C) durable
    4. (D) identical

    正解：(A)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (A) critical mindset 指批判性思維；面對假新聞，讀者需要以批判態度檢視訊息來源與內容。

    ❌ (B) efficient 指有效率的，與辨識假訊息的能力無直接關聯。

    ❌ (C) durable 指耐用的，不能修飾 mindset。

    ❌ (D) identical 指完全相同的，語意不通。

    📚 出處：英文字彙 critical thinking。

  3. 3In this country, employees are ＿＿＿ to retire when they reach the age of 60.

    1. (A) fertile
    2. (B) ignorant
    3. (C) eligible
    4. (D) bulky

    正解：(C)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (C) be eligible to 表示具備資格做某事；年滿 60 歲即符合退休資格，語意相符。

    ❌ (A) fertile 指肥沃的、多產的。

    ❌ (B) ignorant 指無知的，且不與 to retire 搭配。

    ❌ (D) bulky 指笨重龐大的，用於形容物體。

    📚 出處：英文字彙 be eligible to／for。

  16. Doctors without Borders, or Medecins Sans Frontieres (MSF), is an international organization that provides medical assistance to millions of people around the world. The organization was created in 1971 by journalists and doctors in France. Now it works in over 60 countries. Its primary goal is to help people in crisis situations, such as wars, natural disasters, or extreme poverty. MSF raises money from private donors. This allows the organization to work independently from other governments, religions, or militaries and to assist individuals regardless of their nationality or affiliation. With this money, MSF creates medical teams of doctors, nurses, and other health-care professionals. MSF requires these teams to have experience in the medical field and to make a commitment to their work. MSF sends medical teams to countries where there are major needs. For example, a team might go to a refugee camp. Another team might go to a poor country with a lack of doctors and medicines. The teams study the situation and decide what care is needed. MSF can offer many different types of services. During a war, they may provide mental health services. After a natural disaster like an earthquake, they may rebuild hospitals and shelters to help the country recover. While MSF’s medical teams are working in a country, their primary concern is quality medical assistance. However, if a team learns about a new problem, they will speak out so that the international community becomes aware of the situation. MSF will use media, such as newspaper articles or Twitter feeds, to inform the world. For instance, MSF created a campaign called “Starved for Attention.” Global countries were made aware of the issue of childhood malnutrition through this campaign. After MSF has helped a country, they may leave and give its programs to the local citizens of that country. This allows MSF to use its resources to help the next country. However, MSF will not leave a country until the emergency situation has improved. MSF wants to be sure that the country will continue to get medical care after they leave. Through this process, MSF continues to have a positive impact on a community even after the organization has left a country.

    16What is the main purpose of this passage?

    1. (A) To show what MSF most requires.
    2. (B) To explain how MSF operates.
    3. (C) To describe the origins of MSF.
    4. (D) To emphasize the skills of MSF’s doctors.

    正解：(B)

    4. (D) 李四之行為不構成刑法第 163 條第 1 項之公務員縱放或便利脫逃罪，因為提供一支迴紋針，不屬於便利脫逃的行為
