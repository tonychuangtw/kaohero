警察特考　109 年 三等考試 中華民國憲法與消防警察專業英文　考古題與詳解

  1. 1下列基本權，何者受憲法絕對保障，不容國家以任何理由加以侵犯？

    1. (A) 生命權
    2. (B) 人身自由
    3. (C) 思想自由
    4. (D) 傳教自由

    正解：(C)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (C) 依釋字第567號解釋，思想自由保障人民內心精神活動，是人性尊嚴之核心與自由民主憲政之基石，受憲法絕對保障，不容國家以任何理由侵犯或剝奪。

    ❌ (A) 生命權雖屬極重要基本權，但在合憲法定程序下（如依法執行死刑或正當防衛），非不得依法剝奪或限制，屬相對保障。

    ❌ (B) 人身自由受憲法第8條正當法律程序嚴格保障，但在符合法定程序及實體要件下仍得依法逮捕拘禁，屬相對保障。

    ❌ (D) 傳教自由屬於宗教信仰自由之外部表現，得依憲法第23條為維護社會秩序或公共利益而依法予以合憲限制，屬相對保障。

    📚 出處：釋字第567號解釋、憲法第22條、第23條。

  2. 2依憲法增修條文規定，國家肯定多元文化，應積極維護發展下列何者之語言及文化？

    1. (A) 新住民
    2. (B) 邊疆地區各民族
    3. (C) 少數族群
    4. (D) 原住民族

    正解：(D)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (D) 憲法增修條文第10條第11項明定：「國家肯定多元文化，並應積極維護發展原住民族語言及文化。」

    ❌ (A) 憲法增修條文並未明定「新住民」之語言及文化維護，其權益係由法律及政策層面推動保障。

    ❌ (B) 邊疆地區各民族之文化維護係規定於憲法本文第168條，憲法增修條文第10條第11項明定者為原住民族。

    ❌ (C) 憲法增修條文所採用之法規文字為「原住民族」，而非「少數族群」。

    📚 出處：憲法增修條文第10條第11項。

  3. 3依司法院大法官解釋，關於人格權，下列敘述何者錯誤？

    1. (A) 人格權受憲法之保障
    2. (B) 性行為自由無涉人格權之保護
    3. (C) 姓名權為人格權之一種
    4. (D) 刑事羈押涉及人格權之侵害

    正解：(B)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (B) 選項敘述錯誤。依釋字第554號及第791號解釋，性自主權（性行為自由）與人性尊嚴、個人自主密不可分，受憲法第22條人格權所保障，非無涉人格權之保護。

    ❌ (A) 依釋字第399號、第490號等多號解釋意旨，個人之人格權受憲法第22條概括基本權之充分保障。

    ❌ (C) 釋字第399號解釋明確指出，姓名權旨在維護個人主體性，為憲法第22條人格權保障之一種。

    ❌ (D) 釋字第653號及第654號解釋指出，刑事羈押除限制人身自由外，對被告之名譽、隱私等人格權亦造成重大侵害。

    📚 出處：憲法第22條；釋字第399號、第554號、第653號、第791號解釋。

  56. Stop, drop and roll is a simple fire safety technique taught to children, emergency services personnel and industrial workers as a component of health and safety training. Primarily, it is a method to extinguish a fire on a person’s clothes or hair without, or in addition to, the use of conventional firefighting equipment. In addition to extinguishing the fire, stop, drop and roll is thought to be an effective psychological tool, providing those in a fire situation, particularly children, with a routine that can be focused on in order to avoid panic. The technique of stop, drop and roll works for the following reasons: Stop: Running adds oxygen to the fire, or it can be thought of as fanning the flames. Running does not allow for a “smothering” of the fire. It may also prevent bystanders, who may be willing to provide assistance, from smothering the fire with their jackets or other form of clothing, from dowsing the fire with water, or even using a fire extinguisher they might have available. Drop: By dropping to the prone position (face down) you immediately reduce your body surface area that can receive oxygen. In this position the person should immediately cover their face from the flames to prevent burn injury and the difficult disfiguring effects of burned skin. With the body against the ground, it smothers the fire, by reducing the oxygen that is available for the fire. Further, fire burns upward, and by dropping to the prone position, you retard the fire’s ability to burn upward. Roll: Because a person’s clothing (fuel source) circumferences their entire body, the roll portion of the technique ensures that all clothing that may be on fire is smothered (depriving it from oxygen) against the ground, and not just the front or abdominal surface of the body of the prone position. The person should continue “rolling” back and forth until the fire is fully extinguished. Although stop, drop, and roll is a useful technique, some people are skeptical about its true effectiveness. It’s one thing to be so-called “book-smart” about the dos and don’ts of the stop, drop, and roll technique, but it’s another thing to be able to actually perform the technique when your clothes are on fire. You’re feeling the pain of the burn, and you enter panic mode. After all, we have all seen YouTube videos of adults catching fire, and then start running around and flapping their arms in the panic of their situation. With a real and present risk of a child being involved in some type of fire incident, how do we go about trying to teach young children to effectively perform the stop, drop, and roll technique? They may not even really understand what fire is or the danger it presents. They are likely to panic in fear if they catch fire, and their physical capabilities to extinguish themselves may be limited.
