  1. 1依我國憲法規定人民受逮捕拘禁時，逮捕拘禁機關至遲多久應移送該管法院審問？

    1. (A) 48 小時
    2. (B) 36 小時
    3. (C) 24 小時
    4. (D) 12 小時

    正解：(C)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (C) 憲法第 8 條第 2 項明定，人民因犯罪嫌疑被逮捕拘禁時，逮捕拘禁機關至遲應於 24 小時內移送該管法院審問，本人或他人亦得聲請法院於 24 小時內向逮捕機關提審。

    ❌ (A) 48 小時是部分外國立法例採用的期間，我國憲法並未採取。

    ❌ (B) 36 小時並非憲法所定的移送期間。

    ❌ (D) 憲法未將期間縮短為 12 小時；刑事訴訟法第 93 條之 1 另定有法定障礙事由不計入 24 小時，可見基準仍是 24 小時。

    📚 出處：憲法第 8 條第 2 項；提審法第 1 條。

  2. 2依憲法及司法院大法官解釋，下列何者並非憲法上之法官？

    1. (A) 檢察官
    2. (B) 地方法院簡易庭法官
    3. (C) 大法官
    4. (D) 懲戒法院法官

    正解：(A)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (A) 釋字第 392 號指出，檢察官行使的是偵查、訴追與執行等職權，屬廣義司法（司法行政）範圍，並非憲法第 80 條所稱依據法律獨立審判的法官，故不是憲法上之法官。

    ❌ (B) 地方法院簡易庭法官依法獨立行使審判權，當然是憲法上法官。

    ❌ (C) 大法官掌理憲法解釋與憲法審判，釋字第 601 號明白肯認大法官為憲法上之法官，受身分與待遇保障。

    ❌ (D) 懲戒法院（原公務員懲戒委員會）委員行使懲戒審判權，釋字第 162 號認其為憲法上之法官。

    📚 出處：憲法第 80 條；釋字第 392、162、601 號。

  3. 3依司法院大法官解釋，大陸地區人民於臺灣地區設籍未滿 10 年者，不得擔任公務人員之規定，並未牴觸憲法，下列敘述何者錯誤？

    1. (A) 立法者制定規範時應斟酌事物本質的差異而為合理的區別對待，以實現憲法價值
    2. (B) 法律得基於原大陸地區人民對自由民主憲政體制的認識與其他臺灣人民可能不同，限制其任公職之權利
    3. (C) 此類爭議涉及兩岸政治事務，故對於代表民意且充分掌握資訊的立法機關，司法機關應完全尊重其決定
    4. (D) 設籍 10 年始得任公職的限制，仍在必要及合理的範圍

    正解：(C)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (C) 釋字第 618 號雖給予立法者較大的形成空間，但仍以憲法第 7 條平等原則與第 23 條比例原則實質審查該限制是否合理，並未因涉及兩岸政治事務就完全尊重立法決定而放棄違憲審查。

    ❌ (A) 斟酌事物本質差異而為合理區別對待，正是平等原則的內涵，該號解釋明採此立場。

    ❌ (B) 解釋理由書即以原大陸地區人民對自由民主憲政體制的認識可能不同，作為差別待遇的正當理由。

    ❌ (D) 解釋結論認定設籍滿 10 年始得任公職的限制，尚在必要合理範圍內，未違憲。

    📚 出處：釋字第 618 號；憲法第 7、23 條。

At around 10 o’clock this morning, a fishing boat suffered a sudden mechanical failure while sailing in the northeast waters of Taiwan, causing it to lose power, which then caused a fire in the engine room, and the situation was critical. After receiving the report, the Coast Guard Administration immediately dispatched the Wanli ship and speedboat for the rescue and successfully rescued all 8 crew members on board. Two of them were sent to the hospital for treatment due to burns and smoke inhalation. According to preliminary investigations, the fishing boat was conducting ocean-going operations at the time. The captain reported that the engine suddenly vibrated abnormally and then stalled. Subsequently, a large amount of thick smoke came out of the engine room, and the fire spread rapidly in a short period. The crew tried to put out the fire with a fire extinguisher but failed. The fire could not be controlled. Some people jumped into the sea to escape, while some called for help through the emergency radio. After receiving the notification at 10:20, the Coast Guard Administration immediately activated the emergency response mechanism, dispatched the Wanli ship and the Northeast Sea Area patrol boat to the incident area, and contacted the Air Force Command for backup. When the coast guard arrived at the scene at 11:10, smoke was still coming out of the fishing boat’s engine room. Rescuers immediately used water cannons to help cool down the body while launching a search and rescue operation. After about 40 minutes of emergency operations, the three people who fell into the sea and were floating on the sea were successfully rescued. Another five trapped crew members were found on the boat, and all of them were rescued. After a preliminary examination, two crew members had obvious burns and continued coughing, suspected of inhaling excessive smoke. They were immediately sent to Keelung Chang Gung Memorial Hospital by helicopter for emergency treatment. The remaining six people were not seriously injured and have been arranged by the Coast Guard to return to shore to rest. The Coast Guard Administration stated that the detailed cause of the accident still needs to be further clarified. The hull has been secured to prevent further drifting, and special personnel have been arranged to monitor possible subsequent pollution risks. The agency stressed that it will continue to strengthen its emergency response capabilities at sea to ensure maritime safety and efficiency in rescuing lives.
