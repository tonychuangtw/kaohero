  1. 1John is a ＿＿＿ worker. He arrives on time and leaves no mess behind.

    1. (A) conceited
    2. (B) confident
    3. (C) conscious
    4. (D) conscientious

    正解：(D)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (D) conscientious 為形容詞，意為「認真盡責的、一絲不苟的」；後句提到準時且不留髒亂，符合盡責員工的特質。

    ❌ (A) conceited 意為「自負的、自大的」，與後文描述負責任的敬業態度不符。

    ❌ (B) confident 意為「有信心的、自信的」，無法直接呼應後文準時與整潔的工作習慣。

    ❌ (C) conscious 意為「有意識的、清醒的」，通常指生理清醒或注意到的，不形容做事負責。

    📚 出處：核心英文字彙；人格特質形容詞。

  2. 2We will be grateful if you ＿＿＿ receipt of this message at your earliest convenience.

    1. (A) acknowledge
    2. (B) recommend
    3. (C) recognize
    4. (D) suspend

    正解：(A)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (A) acknowledge 意為「確認、承認」；acknowledge receipt of ... 為商務英文常用片語，表示「確認收到（信件或訊息）」。

    ❌ (B) recommend 意為「推薦、建議」，不能與 receipt of message 搭配表示確認收到。

    ❌ (C) recognize 意為「認出、認可」，不符合商務書信中「確認收到信件」的慣用語。

    ❌ (D) suspend 意為「暫停、中止」，與句中感謝對方盡早回覆收件的文意相反。

    📚 出處：核心英文字彙；商務書信常見搭配 acknowledge receipt。

  3. 3Wrong combinations of food can be not only unhealthy but ＿＿＿ .

    1. (A) pointless
    2. (B) tempting
    3. (C) prosperous
    4. (D) toxic

    正解：(D)

    逐題詳解（免費試讀）

    ✅ (D) toxic 意為「有毒的、有害的」；not only unhealthy but toxic 符合語意上的遞進，指錯誤的食物搭配不僅不健康，甚至可能「有毒」。

    ❌ (A) pointless 意為「無意義的、無目的的」，無法與 unhealthy 構成健康層面的語意遞進。

    ❌ (B) tempting 意為「誘人的、吸引人的」，為正向或中性詞，與 unhealthy 語氣矛盾。

    ❌ (C) prosperous 意為「繁榮的、興旺的」，常用於形容經濟或事業，不能形容食物危害。

    📚 出處：核心英文字彙；飲食健康與醫學相關詞彙。

  46. London is one of the top travel destinations in the world, but for a first time visitor, navigating this large city can be overwhelming. Using an Oyster card is an easy way to save time and money if you are planning to use public transportation in London. An Oyster card is a blue credit-card-sized contactless smartcard that can be used on every form of public transportation within London, including public bus, underground, railway, and some river boat services. The Oyster card was first issued on June 30, 2003. The name was decided after a lengthy discussion. Two other proposed names were “Pulse” and “Gem.” “Oyster” was chosen because it sounded like a fresh name that was not directly linked to transportation; also, the hard shell of an oyster and the concealed pearl symbolize security and value. But perhaps the best reason is William Shakespeare’s quote “ The world is your oyster,” which means one has the ability and freedom to do anything or go anywhere. So far more than 60 million cards have been issued and over 85% of rail and bus travel in London is paid by using an Oyster card. The card never expires. It can be used after any length of time. Besides, deposits can be refunded on return of the card. The best reason to get an Oyster card is cost savings and convenience. It is cheaper to travel in London with this card than it is to purchase individual paper tickets for each trip. The card allows 40 people to pass through a ticket gate per minute, 15 more than with paper tickets, and also makes boarding a bus three times faster. For London Underground and the railway system, passengers have to touch the card on an electronic reader when entering and leaving the transport system. The reader will show either a green or a red light. The former indicates that you have sufficient credit for your journey while the latter indicates you do not have enough credit and you have to purchase more credit before you can continue your journey. For the public bus service, which has fixed price, passengers only need to touch the reader at the start of the journey when boarding. If you touch the reader again when you get off the bus, you will be charged an additional fare.
